Method

Weather

Ensemble forecasts from ECMWF IFS ENS (51 members), DWD ICON-EPS (40) and NOAA GEFS (31), fetched every morning from Open-Meteo on a 1° grid of 48 points covering Poland. GEFS has no hub-height wind and is used for solar only; ICON-EPS wind ends after about five days.

Weather to power

  • Solar: pvlib. Hourly irradiance is split into direct and diffuse (Erbs), transposed onto a mix of south-facing and east/west planes (Hay-Davies), corrected for incidence angle and cell temperature (Faiman) and converted with a PVWatts-style model with inverter clipping.
  • Wind: wind speed at 80/100 m is shifted to a 100 m hub height and passed through a generic turbine curve smoothed over the spread of speeds within a grid cell (multi-turbine power curve).
  • Grid points are weighted by public installed capacity per voivodeship: URE register (wind and licensed PV) plus about 14 GW of rooftop PV distributed by population. No individual plant locations are used.

Calibration

For every model and lead day a regression maps the physical output onto PSE generation. It is refitted every day on the last 60 (solar) or 120 (wind) days that were already observed, so it absorbs the fleet's growth, curtailment and model biases. Errors of earlier out-of-sample forecasts, grouped by output level, give the forecast distribution; each ensemble member is dressed with its model's errors, shrunk where the ensemble already spreads widely. Models are weighted by the inverse square of their recent error.

Verification

A rolling-origin hindcast replays the method on archived deterministic runs of the same three models (Open-Meteo Previous Runs API) from 2024, with no look-ahead: each day only data available that morning are used. Scores: MAE of the median, CRPS of the full distribution and the share of observations inside the 80 % range. Archived ensemble members are not available, so the hindcast tests a three-member multi-model ensemble; the live ensemble is verified from the archive of published forecasts as it grows.

Resource climate

Daily ERA5 irradiance and 100 m wind on the same grid since 1991. Anomalies are relative to 1991–2020; national figures weight the grid by installed capacity, maps are voivodeship means. Annual yields use daily transfer functions derived from hourly ERA5 and the same power models, for today's fleet layout and generic equipment, so the year-to-year spread is more meaningful than the absolute level.

Limitations

  • National totals only; regional or plant-level accuracy is not the goal.
  • Curtailment and negative-price shutdowns are only learned statistically.
  • PSE solar figures include an estimate of rooftop generation and are revised.
  • The hindcast starts in mid-2024, so rare extremes are under-sampled.
